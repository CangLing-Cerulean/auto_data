# Conditional diagnosis development, 18 October 2026

Complete retrospective results: 96 historical seed-level responses, 32 decisions, 100 policy trajectories, 260 budget nodes, and four Sol-high signal-generation calls. Independent numerical audits pass. Conditional updates do not improve registered decisions over joint updates or the strong fixed-feature models. Mismatch-driven generation abstains in both source directions; ordinary combinations do not establish decision superiority. No new full model training, cumulative464, no D/test evaluation.

Archive includes all policy snapshots, generation prompts and outputs, transport/failure records, audits, source, and an explicitly preliminary manuscript. `archive_members.json` gives per-member size/SHA256; `release_manifest.json` gives the archive identity. Credentials, private transport configuration, sealed partitions and global research indexes are excluded. Original failures remain visible and are not overwritten by supplementary validation.

This release is a reproducible development result, not a submission-ready empirical claim or independent-source confirmation.
